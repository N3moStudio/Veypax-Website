YOUR FIRST MOVE

Get connected.
Go elsewhere.

This guide explains Veypax setup. Check your device and signing compatibility before purchasing. This page does not distribute an IPA or promise that a signing service supports your device.

01 / Install

  1. Get the IPA for your purchased edition from your seller. This website has no public download or checkout.
  2. Sideload with your signing service. Widgets and Live Activities require Ultimate and its companion extension.
  3. If you have Ultimate, export a backup before replacing an existing installation. For other editions, confirm data-preservation steps with your seller first. Installing over it with the same app identity and signing setup helps preserve its data.

02 / Pair your iPhone

  1. Join Wi-Fi, install LocalDevVPN, and connect it.
  2. Open the app’s setup checklist. The standard tunnel address is 10.7.0.1.
  3. If Pair on this iPhone is offered, follow the in-app instructions. Enter your device passcode only in the iPhone’s own system prompt.
  4. Otherwise, create an RPPairing file with idevice_pair on your computer, then import or paste it into the app. A SideStore pairing file is a different format.
  5. Complete Check connection. Keep pairing files private.

03 / Make your first move

Search for a place, inspect the pin, then tap Teleport. For a journey, Pro supports walking/running/cycling routes; Ultimate also supports driving and e-bike. Review the route before starting. Stop waits support hours, minutes, and seconds, up to 24 hours.

Stop spoofing requests clearing the simulated location. If clearing fails, reconnect and retry. Closing the app does not prove the location was cleared.

Make it yours

With Pro or Ultimate, open Settings → Appearance. Ion uses a dark palette and solid surfaces by default. Choose a different color mode, surface, font, or text size—or use the theme defaults. Every edition uses the same single Veypax icon. Basic uses Glacier with solid surfaces and follows your iPhone’s light/dark mode.

Your edition and updates

Basic includes teleport and favorites. Pro adds joystick, walking/running/cycling routes and appearance customization. Ultimate adds the remaining features. Locked controls explain the required edition; contact your seller to upgrade.

Veypax does not currently have a public update feed. Get verified updates from your seller. Do not enter a private GitHub token into the app or this website.

Optional cloud checks

Ultimate only. These need a separately configured helper and your own authorized provider account. A provider’s latest reported location can be stale. Find My Devices is distinct from Find My People; Find My People is not connected in this version. Keep credentials and helper tokens out of screenshots, backups, and public repositories.
