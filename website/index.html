A LITTLE CLARITY

Good to know.

Does the preview change my location?

No. This website shows an illustration. It does not request your location, connect to your phone, or start a session.

What changes between editions?

Basic covers teleporting and favorites with a fixed Glacier theme, solid surfaces and your iPhone’s light/dark mode. Pro adds joystick control, walking/running/cycling routes and all nine themes. Ultimate adds driving, saved routes and areas, imports and exports, widgets, Live Activities, and optional cloud features. Every edition uses one Veypax icon.

How do upgrades work?

Locked features explain which edition is needed. Contact your seller for an upgraded edition; this website does not process payments or unlock the app. Confirm signing and data-preservation steps before replacing your installation.

What does a verification check tell me?

It shows the location a connected provider reported and how old that report is. An active local session does not guarantee Apple or Life360 has refreshed its report. Find My Devices and Find My People are different; Find My People is unsupported. Only authorized accounts and separately configured helpers are supported; Veypax cannot access arbitrary people’s locations.

Will this website need to keep waking up?

No background app server runs this site. It is designed for static hosting. Optional cloud location helpers are separate services with their own availability and setup.

What happens to my information here?

The appearance preview stays in this page. There are no accounts, analytics scripts, location requests, or cloud credentials. Your host may retain ordinary request logs. Read the website privacy note.
